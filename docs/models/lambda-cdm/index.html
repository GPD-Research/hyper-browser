From 10⁻³⁶ seconds to 10¹¹⁰ years from now, with the Planck 2018 parameters in Lambda_CDM/src/core.py
The sphere is today's observable universe, drawn on a compressed log scale. Its surface shows real sky images where we have them (the CMB maps and Hubble Ultra Deep Field galaxies) and an artist's impression elsewhere. The dark red around it is not space: space is the sphere itself.

Time runs on a log scale because most of the action happens in the first million years. Right of the white line is the future, on a log scale of time after today that is squeezed further past 10¹⁹ years; playback pauses at today. Temperature uses T = T₀/a with T₀ = 2.7255 K. Inflation is drawn as 60 e-folds ending at 10⁻³² s; the physics before ~10⁻¹² s is extrapolated, not tested at those energies. The universe may be much larger than the observable patch shown.
