Small, focused software for problems that off-the-shelf tools do not handle well.

Three projects, each open source and each built around one job. Hyper Browser is released. Magnus and Physics IDE are approaching their first release candidates; their pages describe what they do and where they stand.

  • Hyper Browser Available

    A dual-pane file manager for Android with a built-in photo gallery, a RAW/TIFF inspector, and Google Drive mounted as a pane like any other folder — for reconciling what is on your phone against what is in your cloud.

    About Download the APK Source

  • Magnus Release candidate coming soon

    A highway incident-scene illustrator for Virginia DOT Safety Service Patrol training. Real roadway geometry from OpenStreetMap — lanes, ramps, gores, and markings to MUTCD scale — with a drag-and-drop overlay of trucks, cones, responders, and hazards, audited against SSP procedure.

    About

  • Physics IDE Release candidate coming soon

    A Linux desktop workspace for developing and testing theoretical physics frameworks with two AI research assistants that are given a structured, retrieval-backed awareness of the whole theory rather than a chat window's worth of it.
